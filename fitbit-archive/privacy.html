Privacy policy

Effective September 7, 2026.

This policy describes the personal Fitbit Health Archive operated by Adam Russin. It covers the collector and its archive, and the public information pages under this site's /fitbit-archive/ path.

Data accessed and purpose

After authorization, the collector reads activity and fitness, sleep, and health-metric data through Google Health API read-only permissions. It retains selected daily measurements, dates, units, device/source attribution, and validation information to preserve history and support the owner's personal analysis. It does not modify the source health records.

Storage and service providers

The collector runs in the owner's Google Apps Script project and stores the archive in an owner-controlled Google Sheet. OAuth credentials and tokens are kept in Apps Script property/cache storage as needed for authorized access; they are not included in the public website or exported workbook. Spreadsheet source notes and private execution logs can contain measurement and validation details.

The owner also keeps workbook exports in ChatGPT and may access, maintain, or analyze the archive through authorized ChatGPT connections. Google and OpenAI therefore process the data provided to their respective services under the owner's account settings and their applicable terms and privacy policies. This project does not claim to control those providers' independent account-level retention or data-use settings.

Disclosure and use limits

The archive is intended to remain private to its owner and services the owner authorizes. The project does not publish personal health records, sell health data, or use health data for advertising. It does not operate a separate public account service. Any further sharing is controlled by the owner.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Historical measurements are retained until the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke the app's access through Google Account third-party connections and remove Apps Script triggers to stop collection. Revoking authorization does not delete existing archive copies.

To remove stored data, the owner must delete the relevant Google Sheet, its Legacy Snapshot and source notes, exported workbooks and other retained copies in ChatGPT, and any applicable private logs. Copies in trash, version history, provider backups, or retained conversations follow the relevant service's deletion and retention controls. The owner can also remove the Apps Script project and its stored authorization properties.

This public website

These information pages have no health-data forms, sign-in flow, embedded analytics, or advertising scripts. Requests are handled by GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, which may process connection information such as IP addresses, requested URLs, and security logs according to their own policies and service configuration. Other pages on the personal website may use different features.
