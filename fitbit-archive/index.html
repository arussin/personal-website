Fitbit Health Archive

A private tool that helps its owner keep a historical record of wearable activity, sleep, and recovery measurements in a spreadsheet.

What it does

With the owner's permission, the collector reads Google Health API data associated with Fitbit and Google wearables and records daily measurements in an owner-controlled Google Sheet. Recent dates can be checked again to capture delayed syncs and corrections; older dates can be imported in batches.

Archived measurements include steps, distance, elevation and floors when available, calories, sleep stages, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, and sleep-temperature deviation. Previously archived phone measurements remain separately attributed.

Who can use it

This installation is for its owner's personal use. It does not offer public registration or access to the private archive. This website contains project information only; it does not display health records or initiate authorization.

How data is handled

The collector runs in Google Apps Script. The owner can export spreadsheet copies and use authorized ChatGPT connections to maintain and analyze the archive. Details about access, storage, retention, and deletion are in the privacy policy. Use is described in the terms of service.

Fitbit Health Archive is an independent personal project, not a Google or Fitbit product.
